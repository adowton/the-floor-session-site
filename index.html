Common questions

Before you enquire.

Do we need any dance experience?

None at all. The Floor Session is scaled to the room — from people who've never danced before to the ones who take over the floor. Everyone can follow along.

What if some of the team don't want to join in?

That's completely fine. There's no pressure — people who want to watch, clap and cheer are welcome to. Most reluctant starters end up on the floor within the first ten minutes anyway.

Is it suitable for all fitness levels and ages?

Yes. The session is fully insured and designed to flex to the group — low-impact options are always available alongside the higher-energy choreography.

Where is it held?

At TDC The Dance Company, 214B Hall St, Spotswood — a 2 minute walk from Spotswood Train Station, and just 15 minutes from the CBD (first exit after the West Gate Bridge).

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on group size and package. Enquire with your team size and preferred date and we'll come back with a clear quote — usually within one business day.

What do we need to bring?

Just comfortable clothes and closed shoes. We handle the music, the choreography and the instructor.
